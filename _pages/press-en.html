name

Victor de Paiva. also known as Victor Hugo Ferreira de Paiva (legal name) and otakumaromba. former name: Victor Corrêa. the site says he is not the same person as other people with that name on LinkedIn or in business registries.

short bio

Victor de Paiva is a joguinista, producer, game designer, narrative designer, writer and localizer from Rio de Janeiro, fascinated by games as a means of expression and by the miracle of bringing complex projects until the end. he is Founder & CEO, Garoa Studios, and Product Manager, spawnd.gg (Nuuvem).

long bio

Victor de Paiva is a joguinista, producer, game designer and narrative designer, writer, translator and localizer, publicist, columnist, bizdev, event organizer, mentor, and consultant. a jack-of-all-trades in game development and digital projects, used not only to creative leadership but also to the bureaucratic work nobody else wants to deal with.

he is Founder & CEO, Garoa Studios, a studio focused on experimental and artistic games. the studio has been a finalist several times for best Brazilian game, best narrative, and best audio, and a popular-vote winner at gamescom latam/BIG Festival and SBGames.

the Astro Pig page says Garoa was formally founded, with a name, an identity, and a logo, around May or June 2019. the same page says the team had already been working together since Astro Pig, conceived at the Gamethon game jam inside Hacktudo, on the weekend of 2–4 November 2018, where that team took first place. the people named for that jam are Gabriel Raposo, Igor Carneiro, Pierluigi Piccolo, Carolina Sá, and Victor de Paiva. the about page lists Garoa Studios from 2019 to the present.

he is an occasional columnist at Controles Voadores, a publication dedicated to Brazilian joguinismo, and Product Manager, spawnd.gg (Nuuvem).

he has served as communications director and fiscal council member at ACJOGOS-RJ, operations manager at Game Jam+, and coordinating director at GDP UFRJ. more recently, he has also taken the first steps toward organizing something within the professional community of narrative designers and game writers in Brazil.

languages, as listed on the about page: brasileiro, English, 日本語, русский, and español latam.

work

roles below are the ones each page lists for him. he is not presented here as the sole author of any title.

RhythMania

RhythMania, called RitMania in Portuguese, is a one-button rhythm game in development. the game page lists his roles as executive production, narrative design, localization coordination, marketing, game design, and ux. pages: RhythMania, ritmania.com, Steam.

Astro Pig

Astro Pig is a lo-fi adventure, puzzle, and platformer, released 1 May 2024. the itch.io embed on the game page credits it to Garoa. the page lists his roles as executive production, game design, level design, narrative design, writing, localization, lyrics and vocals, marketing, pr, qa, and ui/ux. the November 2018 jam team named on that page is Gabriel Raposo, Igor Carneiro, Pierluigi Piccolo, Carolina Sá, and Victor de Paiva. page: Astro Pig.

Cartomante – Fortune Teller

Cartomante – Fortune Teller is a comedy and magical realism visual novel, released 1 September 2020. the itch.io embed on the game page credits it to Garoa. the page lists his roles as narrative design, writing, ui/ux, production, social media, pr, marketing, publishing, and localization. page: Cartomante.

Cat Leather Jackets

Cat Leather Jackets is a musical visual novel, released 1 December 2023. the game page credits Diana Amorim for the original concept, music, and programming, Garoa Studios for production, publishing, narrative, QA, and marketing, and Kuating for the art. the page lists his roles as game design, narrative design, writing, production, project management, publishing, qa, marketing, ui/ux, localization, social media, and pr. page: Cat Leather Jackets.

Bagdex

Bagdex is a monster tamer game with RPG, exploration, and turn-based combat. the game page says he worked on it from 2023 to 2025 as narrative designer, writer, and assistant producer, also contributing to game design and marketing. the page does not name a studio. Bagdex is not attributed to Garoa Studios here. page: Bagdex.

Stand by Me

Stand by Me is a partially physics-based puzzle game, released 1 July 2021. the game page says it is based on a prototype by Gabriel Raposo, game designer at Garoa Studios, and the itch.io embed credits the game to Garoa. the page lists his roles as narrative design, writing, executive production, game design, and level design. page: Stand by Me.

Pavu

Pavu is a game in development. the game page lists 2025 as the start of the work period, PC, and a Steam page. it does not state his role or a studio. page: Pavu.

Debtor's Club

Debtor's Club is a narrative resource management game about a debt collector in a corrupt city hall. the game page lists the work period as 2023–2024, PC, Steam, and the Epic Games Store. it does not state his role or a studio. page: Debtor's Club.

spawnd.gg

spawnd.gg is a game discovery platform backed by Nuuvem. he is Product Manager, spawnd.gg (Nuuvem). the site does not present him as the author of the platform.
